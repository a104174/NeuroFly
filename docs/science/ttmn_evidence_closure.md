# Phase 6E — adult TTMn evidence closure and next-scale gate

**Decision (2026-09-24): `CLOSED_NO_EQUIVALENT_TTMN_OBSERVATION_FOUND`.** No suitable identity-resolved adult *Drosophila* TTMn neuronal electrophysiological observation was found in the bounded primary-source and citation-chain set below. This is **not** proof that no such experiment exists. The Phase 6C `ttmn_dimensionless_event_integrator` remains an explicitly exploratory causal model, not a calibrated TTMn neuron. The next scientific focus is **`SENSORY_INDIVIDUALIZATION`**, beginning with an evidence-only MaleCNS column-to-visual-space feasibility gate—not 313-neuron implementation.

Phase 6D's [parameter assessment](ttmn_parameterization_assessment.md), [Phase 6C model](motor_pathway_model.md), and [Phase 6B pathway assessment](motor_escape_feasibility.md) are the baseline. No new evidence here changes their model equation, source identities, parameters, or validation status. The search below targets the remaining *recorded-cell identity* question, rather than repeating a generic GF literature review.

## Search method and bounded citation chain

Starting from King & Wyman (1980) and Tanouye & Wyman (1980), this audit followed the GF/TTMn-relevant references and accessible forward works through Thomas & Wyman (1984), Phelan et al. (1996), Blagburn et al. (1999), Jacobs et al. (2000), Allen & Murphey (2007), Phelan et al. (2008), the Allen & Godenschwege (2010) protocol, von Reyn et al. (2014), Augustin et al. (2017, 2019), and later GF physiology. Searches included `TTMn`, `TTM motoneuron`, `tergotrochanteral motorneuron`, `tergal depressor of trochanter motor neuron`, `TDT motor neuron`, paired recording, intracellular, whole-cell, membrane potential, and coupling coefficient. Publisher pages, PubMed/PMC, author-available primary manuscripts, the official MaleCNS site, and repository-pinned MaleCNS documents were checked. The published 2019 ModelDB code had already been inspected read-only in Phase 6D; it was not rerun here.

The inclusion criterion was an **identified adult TTMn neuronal** voltage/current, AP, or paired GF→TTMn measurement with a specified recording site. GF-neuron recording, TTM-*muscle* recording, other motoneuron recording, developmental dye fill, and computational TTMn voltage were classified separately. Some historical full texts are paywalled or inaccessible; classification below is limited to accessible abstract/methods/figures or the accessible primary manuscript. Search cannot exclude unpublished or unindexed recordings.

## Recording identity ledger

`TTM` is the **tergotrochanteral muscle**; `TTMn` is its **motor neuron**. In particular, an “intracellular TTM recording” commonly means a glass electrode **inside the muscle**, not TTMn.

Further false-positive checks: an adult VNC [whole-cell protocol](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3490319/) targets **MN5/DLMn**, not TTMn; [Godenschwege et al. 2002](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6757528/) records from adult **muscle** while using retrograde TTMn staining; [Bacon & Strausfeld 1986](https://experts.arizona.edu/en/publications/the-dipteran-giant-fibre-pathway-neurons-and-signals/) describes a “motorneuron” signal recorded **extracellularly in muscle** in comparative dipterans, not adult *Drosophila* TTMn membrane voltage. These do not pass the neuronal-equivalence gate. References in the standard protocol to “TTM refractory” or “TTM following” are **GF-stimulation→muscle-output** circuit benchmarks, not isolated TTMn intrinsic refractory/following measurements.

## Calibration closure and frozen semantics

The TTMn calibration path is **CLOSED** until a new *identity-resolved adult TTMn neuronal observation* and a model of the **same** variable, stimulation and recording sites are available. Reopen only after documenting the primary trace or quantitative summary, preparation, sample size, and exact observable. Do not use TTM muscle or leg-extension endpoints as substitutes.

Phase 6C's output remains **TTMn exploratory downstream model state** (`ttmn_dimensionless_event_integrator`): valid for exact-event causal wiring, intervention comparisons, sensitivity, deterministic replay, and explicitly labelled simulated/exploratory visualization. It is invalid as physiological voltage, biological TTMn AP, pair conductance, muscle output, response threshold, or behavior trigger. `tau_motor_ms=10` is an unidentifiable dimensionless-state decay **MODEL_ASSUMPTION**; `event_gain=0.25` is an unidentifiable scale **MODEL_ASSUMPTION**. Neither is tuned here. MaleCNS weights 70/20 remain **chemical structural counts only**; Augustin's 135/34.5 µS values remain model estimates rather than pair-specific MaleCNS conductances. NeuroFly is **NOT EMPIRICALLY VALIDATED**.

**Recommendation: `SENSORY_INDIVIDUALIZATION`.** It offers greater information gain about the move from aggregate/type-level to individual connectome-driven dynamics without pretending the motor pathway has been calibrated. The official [MaleCNS v1.0 download](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/) and [Berg et al. supplemental repository](https://github.com/flyconnectome/2025malecns) were rechecked: the column-type assignment workbook identifies columns/reference photoreceptors, but no documented machine-readable, bilateral MaleCNS column→angular visual-space transform was identified in this bounded check. The official site advertises eyemap browsing; a viewer is not by itself an auditable angle transform. Phase 1D's [D3 finding](receptive_field_feasibility.md) therefore remains in force, subject to a *new, explicit* source/provenance audit if data are released.

**Smallest next phase:** an evidence-only, bounded sensory-registration feasibility review. Seek an official/reproducible column-keyed bilateral angle mapping and matched LC4/LPLC2 receptive-field constraints; verify version, units, eye laterality, orientation, coverage and uncertainty on a small pinned body sample. If unavailable, stop with body-specific visual encoding still disabled. Do **not** implement or render 313 individual neurons in that phase.

Scale gates remain: (1) current 313 structural/model identities with Level P **type-level** encoder input and exact DNp01/TTMn slices; (2) only after validated body-specific stimulus assignment, deterministic controls and identity-preserving artifacts, consider a 313-individual-input milestone; (3) only after that, test larger bounded subgraphs with explicit input, connectivity/modality, parameter and observable validation. None implies readiness for whole-connectome simulation or body behavior.
