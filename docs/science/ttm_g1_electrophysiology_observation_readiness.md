## Anatomical and recording scope

Koenig & Ikeda 2007, Figures 1–3 and Methods, describe 23 TTM fibers. The giant motor axon innervates **G1–G19** via regularly spaced branches; **F1–F4** at the anterior corner receive two finer axons. The G1 fiber is identifiable by position near its tergal attachment, its small regular cylindrical geometry, and the branch between G1/G2. The authors chose it because its simple, extensive innervation permits intracellular recording and estimation of active zones/vesicles. This supports a representative *innervation pattern*, not identical electrical physiology of all G fibers. G1 is one fiber, not a whole-TTM voltage, activation, or force measurement. No studied G1 fiber is linked to MaleCNS body `800146` or `804642`, and study-side laterality is not a resolved crosswalk to those bodies. [Primary Methods and figures](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/full/10.1152/jn.01258.2006)

The 2007 Methods specify **four-day-old adult female *D. melanogaster***: wild-type Oregon-R and temperature-sensitive `shibire^ts1` (`shi`), with some thoracic-`shi` gynandromorph preparations. At 19 °C the mutant recycling mechanism functions; above 27 °C it is impaired, and 29 °C is used for blocked-recycling depletion. A fly was fixed in Tackiwax; a dye-filled glass micropipette entered **G1 near its tergal attachment**, a silver-wire ground entered near the scutellum, and a stimulating electrode was inserted in the neck. Stimulation used 0.1-ms square pulses. Temperature was controlled with a Peltier device and monitored near the fly. The exposed-TTM anatomical preparation used saline of 128 mM NaCl, 4.7 mM KCl, 1.8 mM CaCl₂, and 5 mM Tris-HCl, pH 7.4; the source should be rechecked for the exact bath applying to any particular electrical subexperiment before making a medium-specific comparison. G1 identification is anatomical/positional plus the light/EM branch pattern, **not** a MaleCNS body-ID verification. [Primary Methods](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/full/10.1152/jn.01258.2006)

## Observation versus analysis input

The 2007 Methods apply a Martin RC nonlinear-summation correction. In the paper's notation, `v` is the recorded evoked synaptic potential, `v1` the miniature/quantal potential, `V0` the equilibrium potential used for correction, and `m` the **derived quantal content**. Its appropriateness is argued from G1's long cylindrical form and synapses distributed over its length. Raw EJP amplitude, miniature amplitude, correction input, and resulting quantal count must remain different fields. This assessment does not implement or fit the correction. [Primary Methods and Results](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/full/10.1152/jn.01258.2006)
